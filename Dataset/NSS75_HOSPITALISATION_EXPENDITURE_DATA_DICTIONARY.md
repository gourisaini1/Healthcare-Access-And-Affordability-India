# NSS 75th-round state-level hospitalisation medical expenditure

- **Source:** National Statistical Office, Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, *Key Indicators of Social Consumption in India: Health*, NSS 75th Round (July 2017–June 2018), Table A17, p. A-20.
- **Official survey catalogue:** https://microdata.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/152
- **Published report copy:** https://www.mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/publication_reports/KI_Health_75th_Final.pdf
- **Unit:** average medical expenditure in Indian rupees during hospital stay per hospitalisation case, excluding childbirth.
- **Coverage:** 36 state/UT units as published in 2017–18 geography. Jammu & Kashmir is the pre-2019 combined state; Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu are separate rows. Do not merge these mechanically with current NFHS-5 boundaries.
- **Hospital types:** government/public; private; and all hospitals including charitable/NGO/trust-run.
- **Sectors:** rural, urban and rural+urban combined.
- **Important interpretation:** Table A17 reports average medical expenditure incurred during hospital stay. It is a gross expenditure measure, **not net out-of-pocket expenditure after reimbursements**. Table A21 reports reimbursement percentages at national level by sector/quintile, not state-level net OOPE. Therefore, this dataset must not be described as state-level net OOPE or catastrophic health expenditure.
- **Why included:** it adds a directly observed state-level hospital-spending measure to complement NFHS-5 insurance reach. It does not replace the missing state-level net OOPE analysis.
- **Data handling:** values are transcribed from Table A17 without inflation adjustment. All-hospitals rural+urban is the preferred cross-state headline column; rural and urban values and public/private splits are retained for further analysis.
