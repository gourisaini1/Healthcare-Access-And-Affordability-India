# State-level public health expenditure dataset (RBI Statement 27)

## Coverage
31 States/Union Territories; financial years 2023–24 and 2024–25 (Revised Estimates).

## Indicator
Expenditure on **Medical and Public Health and Family Welfare as a percentage of Aggregate Expenditure**.

## Source
Reserve Bank of India, *State Finances: A Study of Budgets of 2025–26*, Statement 27, “Expenditure on Medical and Public Health and Family Welfare — As per cent of Aggregate Expenditure”, published 23 January 2026.

Official RBI table: https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/PublicationsView.aspx?id=23736

## Fields
- `state_ut`: State or Union Territory name as presented in the RBI table.
- `health_expenditure_share_aggregate_2023_24_pct`: 2023–24 reported value, per cent.
- `health_expenditure_share_aggregate_2024_25_re_pct`: 2024–25 Revised Estimate, per cent.
- `estimate_type_2024_25`: Status of the 2024–25 observation.

## Interpretation and limitations
1. 2024–25 observations are **Revised Estimates (RE)**, not final audited accounts or actual expenditure. They are not Budget Estimates (BE).
2. The indicator combines Medical and Public Health with Family Welfare. It is a share of aggregate state expenditure, not expenditure as a percentage of GSDP and not per-capita expenditure.
3. It measures budgetary expenditure composition, not service quality, access, household affordability, or health outcomes.
4. The RBI notes that data from 2017–18 onwards include Delhi and Puducherry. Telangana series begins in 2014–15; earlier values are not applicable.
5. The all-States-and-UTs aggregate row is not included in this state-level file.
6. Comparisons should account for differences in state fiscal structures and the RE status of the latest year.
