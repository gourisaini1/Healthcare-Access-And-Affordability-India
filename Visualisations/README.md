## Reading the figures

- A financing share describes the composition of health expenditure, not the distribution of household financial hardship.
- A budget share describes the composition of state expenditure, not spending per resident, expenditure as a share of GSDP or service quality.
- The rural PHC staffing ratio uses the source-reported administrative requirement as its denominator. It is not a population-need measure or doctors per capita.
- Government bed counts exclude private capacity and do not measure occupancy, staffing, geographic access or quality. The population-adjusted rate combines reference dates about 21 months apart.
- The adjusted regression plot compares two different staffing scales. The linear and log coefficients should not be compared as if their units were identical. The log-ratio model is the preferred specification because the raw ratio is right-skewed; the full-sample linear model is retained as a sensitivity check.

All figures are descriptive or exploratory. See the corresponding notebook, source CSV and data dictionary for definitions, sample coverage and caveats. Re-run the notebooks in numerical order to regenerate the charts.
